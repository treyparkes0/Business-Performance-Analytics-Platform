Business Performance & Strategy Analytics Platform

A business analytics project that evaluates the performance of six technology companies—Microsoft, Apple, NVIDIA, AMD, Adobe, and CrowdStrike—across revenue growth, profitability, operating efficiency, cash generation, and financial health.

The project uses Python, SQL, and Databricks to collect, prepare, structure, and analyze financial data from SEC filings. Reusable SQL views are used to evaluate company performance, identify trends, compare companies against peers, and highlight areas that may warrant management attention.

The analysis is presented through an interactive Streamlit application that allows users to explore company performance, compare key business metrics, review financial trends, and identify management attention area
